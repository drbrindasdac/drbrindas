Treatments Offered
Treatments

Everything your smile needs, under one roof.

From a routine cleaning to full-mouth rehabilitation — planned carefully, explained plainly, priced honestly.

Dental Implants

Permanent, natural-looking replacement for missing teeth. Guided placement, titanium fixtures, lifetime of chewing confidence.

Clear Aligners

Straighten teeth invisibly with Aesthalign. No metal brackets, removable for meals, scanned and planned digitally.

Smile Design

Veneers, contouring and whitening designed around your face — previewed digitally before a single tooth is touched.

Root Canal Treatment

Single-sitting RCT under magnification. Saves the natural tooth, and patients consistently describe it as painless.

Oral Surgery

Wisdom tooth removal, extractions and minor surgical procedures — done cleanly, with quick, comfortable recovery.

Preventive & Kids Care

Cleanings, fluoride, sealants and gentle paediatric visits. The cheapest dentistry is the treatment you never need.
